Common Error Types

Sign error
Dropping a negative sign when expanding or differentiating, e.g. writing +3 instead of −3.
Power rule slip
Applying the wrong exponent: writing 3x² instead of 2x³ when differentiating x³, or similar.
Coefficient copy error
Silently changing a coefficient mid-working, e.g. 5 becomes 3.
Forgotten +C
Omitting the constant of integration in an indefinite integral.
Normal gradient
Using the tangent gradient instead of −1/m for the normal line.
Rewrite error
Incorrectly converting roots or fractions to index form before calculus.
Arithmetic slip
A wrong substitution or evaluation, e.g. 2³ = 6 instead of 8.
Precision / format
Ignoring the required number of decimal places or significant figures.
